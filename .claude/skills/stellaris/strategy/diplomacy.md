# Diplomacy & Leaders Guide (Stellaris 4.3 Cetus)

## Opinion & Relations

Opinion ranges -1500 to +1500. Improving relations:
- Envoy "Improve Relations": +0.25/month (caps +150)
- One-sided favorable trade deals: up to +100 opinion
- Matching ethics: permanent positive modifier
- Trust builds from active treaties (research agreement, NAP, defensive pact)

Threat decays ~0.25/year. NAP with an empire halves their perceived threat from you. High threat causes defensive coalitions.

## Federations

### Best Types
- **Research Cooperative**: Universally strong. Tech sharing + research bonuses compound over time. Form early.
- **Martial Alliance**: Best for crisis preparation or coordinated conquest.
- **Trade League**: Strong for tall/economic empires with developed trade networks (mid-game).
- **Hegemony**: Dominant empire controls all. Powerful but risky -- members rebel if you weaken.

### Timing
Research Cooperative: form as early as possible. Trade League: once trade network developed. Martial Alliance: when crisis or major war is coming.

## Vassals

### Specialist Types (Overlord DLC)
- **Prospectorium**: Tithes basic resources (minerals, energy, food)
- **Scholarium**: Tithes research -- great for tech edge
- **Bulwark**: Defensive buffer, strengthens military
- **Tributary**: 100% independent, gives resources with no control needed

**Best combo**: Prospectorium (basic resources) + Scholarium (research) -- complementary loop.

**Peaceful vassalization**: Maximize opinion + be massively more powerful. Offer lenient terms initially, tighten later.

## Galactic Community

### Best Resolutions
- **Industrial Development**: Worker output and resource bonuses
- **Unchained Knowledge**: Excellent research bonuses
- **Galactic Commerce**: Good trade benefits (reduces naval cap as trade-off)
- **Defense/War resolutions**: Generally poor value, avoid

### Diplomatic Weight
40% from Technology, 15% from Economy, 2.5% from Fleet Power, rest from Population (happy pops count 3x). Envoys assigned: +10% each.

### Custodian Path
+5 influence/month, +30% diplomatic weight, can end sessions early, propose unique reforms, access GDF. Generally better than Galactic Imperium (which risks galactic war).

## Espionage

Assign envoy as spymaster -> infiltration grows +1/day. Key operations:
- 10: Gather Information
- 30: Prepare Sleeper Cells (envoy can leave)
- 40: Steal Technology (gain random tech target has)
- 45: Sabotage Starbase
- 60: Arm Privateers (spawn pirate fleets in target territory)

Assets provide +4 skill bonus per matching category. Acquire at 30 infiltration.

## Leader Management

### Three Classes
- **Commander**: Fleets, armies
- **Scientist**: Science ships, research
- **Official**: Governance, diplomacy

### Key Traits
- **Spark of Genius**: +3%/+6% research speed
- **Unifier**: +1% Unity from jobs per level
- **Eye for Talent**: +5% Leader XP for all leaders (council)
- **Field Expertise III**: +50% research speed for that field

### Veteran Classes (Level 4, Paragons DLC)
Choose specialization that unlocks veteran-specific trait pool. Level 8: Destiny Trait (most powerful).

### 4.3 Changes
- Aptitude tradition reworked: +2% specialist efficiency per governor level, +1 starting traits
- Unifier no longer scales with leader level (significant nerf)
- Corporate Authority grants +1 Envoy instead of +1 External Leader Pool

## Council Positions

### Top Civic Council Positions
1. **Lord Commander** (Crusader Spirit): +1% Ship Fire Rate per level
2. **Divine Conduit** (Divine Sovereign): +1% Resources from Psionic Pops per level
3. **Speaker of Parliament** (Parliamentary System): +3% Council Agenda Speed per level
4. **Pharmaceutical Executive** (Pharma State): +1% Pop Growth per level

### 4.3 Changes
- Technocracy council: no longer reduces Researcher upkeep; provides cross-disciplinary research
- Distinguished Admiralty: +20% Command Limit (percentage instead of flat)

## Government Reforms

20-year cooldown, costs 10 Unity per Empire Size. Key windows:
- **Mid-game (2300-2350)**: Switch from expansion to economy/military civics
- **Pre-crisis (2350-2400)**: Military-focused or alloy-boosting civics
- Democracy resets reform cooldown on elections (more flexible)

## Crisis Diplomacy

- Build relationships early -- can't forge alliances during crisis
- Pursue Custodian for GDF access
- Priority resolutions: GDF 1, Galactic Mobilization, GDF 2, United Front
- War in Heaven: all combat becomes Total War, Containment CB against Awakened Empires

### 4.3
All crisis fleets reduced ~1/3, Fallen Empires ~1/2. Multiple crises can now spawn 25 years apart instead of being blocked.
