# Expansion & Colonization Guide (Stellaris 4.3 Cetus)

## When to Colonize

- First colony ship as soon as possible (within first 5-10 years)
- Colonize guaranteed habitable worlds first (2 planets near homeworld)
- Don't wait for "perfect" planets -- pops are your ultimate resource
- 4.3: Empire size from pops halved, but empire size per colony increased to 20

## Planet Evaluation Criteria

### Priority Factors (in order)
1. **Size**: Larger planets = more district slots = more pops. Size 20+ is excellent, 15+ is good.
2. **Habitability**: 60%+ is workable, 80%+ is ideal. Low habitability reduces job output and pop growth.
3. **Planet class**: Match to species preference. Continental/Tropical/Ocean are common. Gaia worlds have 100% habitability for everyone.
4. **Planetary features**: Rich deposits (d_rich_mountain, d_lush_jungle) add resources. Rare resource deposits are valuable.
5. **Blockers**: Fewer is better early game (clearing costs society research + time). Some blockers transform into features when cleared.
6. **Strategic position**: Chokepoint systems, resource-rich systems, forward bases.

### Planet Classes Worth Colonizing
- **Gaia**: Always colonize (100% habitability for all species)
- **Relic**: Excellent if you can restore it (Remnants origin)
- **Tomb**: Only if you have Tomb World preference or Post-Apocalyptic origin
- **Matching preference**: Your species' preferred class
- **Non-matching habitable**: Still worth it for size 18+ with good features

## Colonization Priorities

1. Guaranteed habitable worlds (near homeworld)
2. Large (18+) high-habitability planets in claimed systems
3. Planets with rare resource deposits
4. Strategic chokepoint systems (even small planets for starbase placement)
5. Habitats (mid-game, Void Dwellers)

## Chokepoint Strategy

- Identify natural chokepoints (systems with single hyperlane connections)
- Claim and fortify these before expanding further
- Build defensive starbases at chokepoints, anchorage starbases behind them
- A single fortified chokepoint can protect an entire sector

## Influence Management

- Base: 3 influence/month
- Factions: up to +2-3 additional (keep factions happy)
- Rivalries: +0.5 each (up to 3 rivals)
- Power projection: scales with fleet power relative to cap
- Costs: Claims (25-75 per system), Starbases (75), Colony ships (implied via alloys), Edicts

### Saving Influence
- Don't over-claim -- only claim what you'll take in the next war
- Use Interstellar Dominion perk (-20% claim/starbase cost)
- Frontier starbases are cheaper than claiming then building

## Sector Management

- Sectors auto-form around colony planets (4 hyperlane radius)
- Assign governors for bonuses (+stability, +resource output based on traits)
- Sector capitals get designation bonuses
- Don't micromanage sectors you can't keep up with -- focus on key planets

## Wide vs Tall

**Wide is strictly superior in 4.3**:
- More pops = more of everything
- Empire size penalty from pops was halved
- Colony empire size increased to 20 but is offset by the pop gains
- Tech rush + wide expansion is the dominant strategy
- "Tall" only viable for roleplay or specific challenge runs

## Habitat Expansion (Mid-Game)

- Requires Voidborne ascension perk or Void Dwellers origin
- x1.5 specialization multiplier
- Build in systems with orbital resource deposits
- Good for: research, alloy production, strategic resource extraction
- Cheap compared to colonizing distant planets

## Megastructure Expansion (Late-Game)

### Priority Build Order
1. **Dyson Sphere/Swarm**: Energy independence
2. **Matter Decompressor**: Mineral foundation
3. **Science Nexus**: Research acceleration
4. **Ring Worlds**: The best "planets" in the game (x5 specialization)
5. **Strategic Coordination Center**: Naval capacity + bonuses

### Kilostructures (Mid-Game)
- **Dyson Swarm**: Amplifies star energy up to 30x (3-energy star = 90 energy)
- **Arc Furnace**: Built on molten worlds for minerals (-100 energy upkeep)

## Planet Specialization After Colonization

1. **Immediately set designation** based on the planet's strengths
2. Build only what the designation supports
3. First buildings: efficiency buildings for the primary job type
4. Grow pops before building more districts
5. Specialize districts when gateway techs are researched

### Common Specializations
- **Forge World**: Best planets with minerals access nearby
- **Tech World**: High-habitability planets with research deposits
- **Generator World**: Systems with energy deposits
- **Mining World**: Systems with mineral deposits
- **Agri World**: One is usually enough for the whole empire (biological)

## Dealing with Hostile Systems

- **Marauders**: Pay tribute (500 energy) if threatened early. Don't provoke until mid-game fleet is ready.
- **Leviathans**: Mark system, return with 10k+ fleet power. Killing them often gives unique rewards.
- **Space fauna**: Expand around them early, clear later when fleet is stronger.
- **4.3 note**: Space fauna unchanged while player power reduced -- they're relatively more dangerous.
