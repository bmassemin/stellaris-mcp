# Military Guide (Stellaris 4.3 Cetus)

## Naval Capacity (4.3)

Ships use ~5x their previous naval capacity. Individual ships matter more, losses hurt more.

| Ship Class | Naval Cap |
|---|---|
| Corvette | 5 |
| Frigate | 8 |
| Destroyer | 10 |
| Cruiser | 20 |
| Battleship | 40 |
| Titan | 80 |
| Juggernaut/Colossus | 100 |
| Base capacity | 50 |

Sources: Anchorages (+5 each, +3 with Naval Logistics Office), Soldiers (now Specialist jobs), Repeatable tech (+20/level), Supremacy tradition (+20%).

## Fleet Composition by Phase

**Early game**: 20-40 corvettes, mix interceptor + missile boat configs. Missiles needed for starbase takedowns.

**Mid game**: Carrier Cruisers are the best pre-Battleship ship. 100% accuracy strike craft hard-counter corvettes. Mix carrier (hangar core) + DPS cruisers (L/M slots).

**Late game**: ~80% Artillery Battleships + ~20% Picket Destroyers for point defense. Corvette spam is dead in 4.3.

**Frigates** (new niche in 4.3): Faster than before (Destroyer speed), +1 Utility/Aux slot, Destroyer-sized reactors. Viable gap-filler between corvettes and destroyers.

## Ship Design

### 4.3 Critical Change
AI now routinely has 50% shield/armor hardening. Bypass weapons (Arc Emitter) are no longer dominant -- you need combined arms.

### Battleship Builds
- **General purpose**: Tachyon Lance/Giga Cannon (spinal) + Kinetic Artillery (L) + Enigmatic Decoders (aux)
- **Anti-armor**: Tachyon Lance + Neutron Launchers
- **Anti-shield**: Giga Cannon + Kinetic Artillery

### Destroyer Build
Picket bow + Flak Artillery in all PD slots. Flak preferred over Guardian PD (higher tracking negates missile evasion).

### Shields vs Armor
- Armor has 33% more HP than equivalent shields but no regen
- Higher-tier components add (tier-1)% hardening
- Against AI: armor generally stronger (AI favors energy weapons)

### Disruptors (4.3)
50% shield+armor bypass at T1, scaling to 80% at Psionic tier. Still strong but less dominant due to widespread AI hardening.

## War Timing

- Strike when you hit a tech timing (tier 2 reactors/weapons/shields)
- ~30-40 corvettes for a first war against equivalent neighbor
- Attack when AI fleets are split or away from starbases
- Pre-claim every system before declaring war

## War Exhaustion
- 0% to 100%, from ship losses, ground losses, planet destruction, passive time
- At 100%: forced status quo after 24 months
- Minimize your losses aggressively; kill enemy ships to push them over

## Starbase Defense

- Ion Cannons are the premier defensive weapon (Perdition Beam equivalent)
- Up to 3 Ion Cannons per starbase (8 defense platform slots each)
- Always keep a fleet nearby -- AI won't attack starbases it expects to lose to, but will avoid them if no fleet backup

### 4.3 Changes
- Weapon modules no longer increase defense platform cap
- Deep Space Citadel utility slots reduced from 8 to 6 per tier
- Starbases now have innate shields equal to armor

## Titan Auras
- **Offensive (system-wide, prioritize)**: Shield Dampener + Fire Rate Reduction
- Don't stack same aura -- spread different offensive auras across titans
- Recommended: 1 titan reducing fire rate + 1 titan dampening shields

## Crisis Counter-Builds

### Prethoryn Scourge
- Weapons: Energy (they have no shields). Tachyon Lance + Missiles + Strike Craft
- Defense: Full armor, no shields

### Unbidden (Extradimensional)
- Weapons: Kinetic (they have zero armor). Giga Cannon + Kinetic Artillery
- Defense: Maximum shields (their weapons are energy-based)

### Contingency
- Weapons: Arc Emitter + Cloud Lightning (low hull HP)
- **Remove Sapient Combat AI from all ships** (Contingency debuffs it)
- Defense: Shields primarily

### Fallen Empires
- Arc Emitter + Cloud Lightning + Disruptors (bypass massive armor/shields)
- Alternative: Long-range artillery battleships
- Repeatable tech stacking is essential

## 4.3 Crisis Power
All crisis fleets reduced by ~1/3. Fallen Empire fleets reduced by ~1/2. But player power decreased more -- crises are relatively harder.
