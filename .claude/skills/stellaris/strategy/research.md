# Research & Technology Guide (Stellaris 4.3 Cetus)

## Tech System Basics

Card-draw system: 3 techs offered per draw (expandable). 3 research areas, 13 subcategories. 6 tiers (T0-T5) -- moving to each new tier requires 6 techs from the preceding tier in the same area.

4.3 halved tech costs to compensate for reduced research production. Net effect: similar tech speed but tighter economies.

## Early Priorities (First 50 Years)

### Physics
1. Administrative AI (+5% research speed empire-wide)
2. Exotic Materials Labs (Research Complexes, Data-Driven Theorem Facility)

### Engineering
1. Powered Exoskeletons -> Artificial Workforce (Robot Assembly Plants -- critical for pop growth)
2. Artificial Specialists (rare, T2 -- lets robots take specialist jobs including researchers)

### Society
1. Genome Mapping (+10% pop growth speed)
2. Colonial Centralization (capital upgrades)
3. Ascension Theory (rare -- extra ascension perk + ambition edicts)

## Research Alternatives (Beelining)

- Default: 3 cards per draw
- Tech from previous hand has halved weight in next draw
- Need 4+ alternatives as "bare minimum for beelining"
- Match scientist expertise to target field for both speed and draw weight bonuses

### Sources of +1 Alternatives
- Science Division tradition (Discovery tree)
- Self-Aware Logic technology
- Inquisitive veteran trait

### Weight Multipliers
| Modifier | Effect |
|---|---|
| Expertise III scientist | x1.75 |
| Technological Ascendancy perk | x1.5 for rare techs |
| Research Cooperative L3 federation | x2 if ally knows tech |
| Rare tech base weight | x0.125 (one-eighth normal) |

## Scientist Traits

**Best for research speed**: Spark of Genius (+3%/+6%), Hyper Focus (+5%/+8%/+10%)

**Best for beelining**: Field Expertise (I: +15% speed, +25% draw chance; II: +30%, +35%; III: +50%, +75%)

**Technocracy**: Scientists gain random expertise traits automatically. In 4.3, council position provides cross-disciplinary research.

## Traditions Priority (Tech Rush)

1. **Discovery** (always first): +1 Research Alternatives, +10% research speed finisher
2. **Expansion** (growth): +1 extra pop on colony, +10% pop growth
3. **Prosperity** (economic foundation)
4. **Supremacy** (when military pressure arrives)
5. **Aptitude** (reworked in 4.3): +2% specialist efficiency per governor level, +1 leader starting traits

## Ascension Perks Tier List

### S-Tier
- **Technological Ascendancy** (T0): +50% rare tech chance. Almost always first pick.
- **Galactic Wonders** (T0): Science Nexus, Dyson Sphere, Ring Worlds, Matter Decompressor.

### A-Tier
- **Transcendent Learning** (T0): +2 scientist capacity, +25% leader XP
- **Imperial Prerogative** (T0): -25% empire size from planets
- **Master Builders** (T2): +50% megastructure build speed, +1 build capacity

### Ascension Paths (pick one)
- **Synthetic Evolution**: Best for pure tech rush; robots can be researchers
- **Cybernetics**: Buffed in 4.3 (no energy upkeep, new positive traits)
- **Psionics**: Nerfed slightly (Zro deficit penalties)
- **Biological**: Nerfed (organic efficiency bonuses removed)

## Repeatable Techs

### Best Repeatables
- **Physics**: Energy output, energy weapon fire rate/damage (cleanest tree)
- **Engineering**: Mineral output, kinetic weapon fire rate/damage, hull/armor
- **Society**: "+1 Core Worlds" (single best repeatable for late-game)

Naval capacity repeatable: +20 per level in 4.3 (matches 5x naval cap increase).

### Pool Management
- Skip techs that lead to undesirable repeatable chains
- 4+ alternatives helps avoid bad repeatables
- Debris-salvaged techs bypass normal prerequisites

## Tech Rush Build (4.3)

| Component | Choice |
|---|---|
| Authority | Oligarchy or Democracy |
| Ethics | Fanatic Materialist + Egalitarian |
| Civics | Technocracy + Meritocracy |
| Origin | Prosperous Unification (safe) or Remnants (aggressive) |
| Species | Intelligent, Natural Physicists/Engineers, Conservationist |

**Opening**: Specialize homeworld for research -> Rush Discovery -> First perk: Technological Ascendancy -> Rush robotics -> Expand to guaranteed habitables.

**Wide > Tall in 4.3**: More pops = more researchers. Empire size penalty from pops was halved. Wide expansion is strictly superior.
